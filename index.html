Our Story

Built by the Movement, For the Movement

VolCon began with a simple but powerful belief: Volunteering deserves a space of its own.

A space not just to show up, but to learn, connect & shape the future of volunteering in India.

This vision first came alive when Bhumi and AID India joined hands to create a national platform dedicated entirely to volunteering. At a time when volunteer efforts were powerful but scattered, VolCon brought people together.

What began as an experiment soon became something much bigger. As the movement grew, so did the collective leadership behind it. iVolunteer joined as a co-organiser, strengthening the vision of VolCon as a platform owned not by one organisation, but by the ecosystem itself.

"Volunteers, nonprofit leaders, CSR professionals, and social impact practitioners didn’t just attend; they began to shape VolCon. They curated conversations. They influenced themes. They defined what mattered."

Since 2020, Bhumiversal has produced VolCon, helping scale its ambition and impact. Bringing together global insights, cross-sector collaboration, and deeper conversations on the future of volunteering. But the real turning point came in 2024 when the community itself stepped forward.

Today, VolCon stands as India’s flagship volunteering conference, where ideas spark action, partnerships are born, and volunteers discover their power to create change.

Corporate Committee

To maximise participation and learning of corporate participants.

  • • Determining agenda based on corporate volunteer needs
  • • Identifying and enlisting speakers for corporate tracks
  • • Maximising employee volunteer leader attendance
Skills: Strong corporate network, CSR understanding, teamwork & decision making.

NGO Committee

To maximise participation and learning of NGO participants.

  • • Determining NGO agenda based on grassroots needs
  • • Enlisting speakers engaging volunteers at scale
  • • Driving nationwide NGO participation
Skills: Grassroots network, understanding NGO volunteer challenges, communication.

Program Committee (7–9 Members)

To ensure every participant has a great conference experience.

  • • Overall plan: plenary sessions, stalls & side events
  • • Pre/post communication, welcome kits & surveys
  • • Interactive games & WhatsApp engagement
Skills: Event design, large team engagement, attention to detail.

Continuous Learning

To sustain learning and capacity building year-round.

  • • Curation of toolkits, insights, and research papers
  • • Structuring post-conference knowledge sharing
  • • Connecting alumni cohorts across editions
Skills: Research affinity, curriculum curation, mentoring.

Finance Committee

To ensure fiscal responsibility and sponsorship stewardship.

  • • Budget allocation and venue logistics budgeting
  • • Supporting corporate sponsorship outreach
  • • Managing scholarship fund disbursements
Skills: Budget management, corporate development, governance.

Marketing Committee

To amplify VolCon's mission across media & youth channels.

  • • Outreach campaigns for attendee registration
  • • Storytelling, volunteer spotlights & PR
  • • Social media and partner network outreach
Skills: Digital marketing, campaign leadership, media ties.

Structure and Working of the Committees

  • 5 Members: Each committee will have five members who bring relevant domain expertise.
  • Chairperson: Led by a chairperson (preferably a previous VolCon member) who oversees activities and facilitates meetings.
  • Members: Selected based on qualifications or appointed by relevant stakeholders.
  • Roles & Responsibilities: Task execution, research, decision-making, and reporting.
  • Meeting Schedule: Regular meetings to discuss progress, address challenges, and coordinate.

Expectations, Benefits & Rewards

  • Commitment: ~2 hours/week (may increase closer to event date).
  • Meetings: Attend at least 75% of meetings, establish agendas, and share minutes.
  • Networking: Connect with peers across committees and sector leaders.
  • Exposure: Immerse in innovative ideas, best practices, and industry trends.
  • Participation: Subject to sufficient sponsorship, participation could be partially or fully covered.
